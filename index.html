1. Data Fiduciary & Scope

This platform (operated independently by ShaktiQ at https://shaktiq.com/) acts as the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India). We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of every competitive exam aspirant using our PYQ Bank and Mock Test Series.

2. Personal Data Collected & Purpose Limitation

When you sign in to unlock the 2,800+ PYQ Question Bank and 22 Full-Length Mock Tests, we collect only the minimum necessary personal data with your explicit affirmative consent:

  • Full Name & Email Address: To create your aspirant profile and associate your mock test scorecards and bookmarks.
  • 10-Digit Mobile / WhatsApp Number: To verify unique aspirant registrations and share important exam/test updates if consented.
  • Target Exam: To tailor Bihar-specific exam preparation analytics (72nd/73rd BPSC CCE, BSSC CGL 4, AEDO, TRE).
  • Mock Test Scores & Question Attempts: To generate your accuracy breakdown and performance history.

3. Data Storage, Google Sheets Sync & Retention

Your profile and test progress are stored locally in your browser (localStorage and IndexedDB) for instant offline-capable access and are transmitted securely over HTTPS to our secure Google Sheets registration & scorecard log via Google Apps Script. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party marketing agency or coaching institute. Data is retained only for as long as your profile remains active or until you withdraw your consent.

4. Your Rights as a Data Principal (Sections 11–14, DPDP Act 2023)

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can view and export your complete profile and test records as a JSON file at any time from your Profile dialog.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You can update your details or permanently erase your local profile and withdraw consent using the button below.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You may contact our Grievance Officer at contact@shaktiq.com for any data removal or privacy request.

BPSC 68th CCE Prelims • Modern History & Bihar Target: 72nd/73rd BPSC

During the 1857 Revolt in Bihar, which British commander was defeated by Veer Kunwar Singh near Jagdishpur on 23rd April 1858?

Correct Answer: B (Captain Le Grand)

BPSC Trap Alert: 23 अप्रैल 1858 को वीर कुंवर सिंह ने कैप्टन ली ग्रैंड (Captain Le Grand) को पराजित किया था। जबकि मेजर विन्सेंट आयर (Vincent Eyre) ने अगस्त 1857 में जगदीशपुर पर कब्जा किया था। इसी विजय की स्मृति में हर साल 23 अप्रैल को बिहार सरकार 'विजयोत्सव' मनाती है।

BSSC CGL Pattern • General Science (Physics & Optics) Secretariat Assistant

Which optical phenomenon is primarily responsible for the twinkling of stars observed from Earth's surface?

Correct Answer: A (Atmospheric Refraction)

BSSC Elimination Trick: तारों का टिमटिमाना वायुमंडलीय अपवर्तन (Atmospheric Refraction) के कारण होता है क्योंकि वायुमंडल की विभिन्न परतों का घनत्व और अपवर्तनांक निरंतर बदलता रहता है। प्रकाश का प्रकीर्णन (Scattering) आकाश के नीले रंग के लिए जिम्मेदार है, न कि टिमटिमाने के लिए।

AEDO Special • Bihar Panchayati Raj Act 2006 Rural Development

Under the Bihar Panchayati Raj Act, 2006, what percentage of reservation was granted to women across all three tiers of Panchayats?

Correct Answer: B (50% Reservation)

AEDO Key Highlight: बिहार पंचायती राज अधिनियम, 2006 के तहत बिहार भारत का पहला राज्य बना जिसने पंचायती राज संस्थाओं के सभी स्तरों (ग्राम पंचायत, पंचायत समिति और जिला परिषद) में महिलाओं को 50% आरक्षण प्रदान किया। 73वें संविधान संशोधन में न्यूनतम 33% आरक्षण का प्रावधान है।

CONCISE NOTES • RAPID REVISION SHEETS

High-Yield Exam Fact Sheets

Structured one-liner revision tables distilled from published BPSC, BSSC CGL 4 & AEDO papers for quick last-minute revision.

Geography & State Profile

Bihar Administrative & River Facts

  • • Divisions & Districts: 9 Divisions, 38 Districts (38th: Arwal), 101 Sub-divisions, 534 Blocks.
  • • Ganga River Length in Bihar: 445 km (Longest in Patna district: 99 km; enters at Chausa, Buxar).
  • • North Bihar Tributaries: Ghaghara, Gandak, Burhi Gandak (fastest flow), Bagmati, Kamla, Kosi ("Sorrow of Bihar"), Mahananda.
  • • South Bihar Tributaries: Karmnasa, Sone (joins at Danapur/Maner), Punpun (joins at Fatuha), Phalgu, Kiul, Ajay.
Census 2011 Benchmark

Demographic High-Frequency Numbers

  • • Population Density: 1,106 per sq km (1st in India; Highest: Sheohar 1,880; Lowest: Kaimur 488).
  • • Literacy Rate: 61.80% (Male: 71.20%, Female: 51.50%). Highest: Rohtas (73.37%); Lowest: Purnia (51.08%).
  • • Sex Ratio: 918 females/1000 males (Child Sex Ratio: 935). Highest: Gopalganj (1,021); Lowest: Munger (876).
  • • Decadal Growth (2001–11): 25.42% (Highest: Madhepura 31.12%; Lowest: Gopalganj 19.02%).
Sanctuaries & GI Tags

Ecology, Ramsar & GI Tags

  • • Valmiki Tiger Reserve: West Champaran (Only National Park & Tiger Reserve in Bihar).
  • • Key Ramsar Sites: Kanwar Lake (Kabartal, Begusarai — 1st Ramsar site of Bihar), Nagi & Nakti Bird Sanctuaries (Jamui).
  • • Famous GI Tags: Mithila Makhana, Shahi Litchi (Muzaffarpur), Jardalu Mango (Bhagalpur), Katarni Rice, Magahi Paan, Marcha Rice (West Champaran), Madhubani Painting, Sujini & Sikki Grass Craft.
1857 Revolt in Bihar

Chronology & Veer Kunwar Singh

  • • 12 June 1857 (Rohini, Deoghar): Revolt began in Bihar Presidency (32nd Infantry Regiment).
  • • 3 July 1857 (Patna): Led by bookseller Pir Ali Khan (Commissioner: William Tayler).
  • • 25 July 1857 (Danapur Cantt): 7th, 8th & 40th Regiments mutinied and joined Veer Kunwar Singh at Jagdishpur.
  • • 23 April 1858: Kunwar Singh defeated British force led by Captain Le Grand near Jagdishpur.
Gandhian Era & Peasant Movements

Champaran (1917) & Kisan Sabha

  • • Champaran Satyagraha (1917): Rajkumar Shukla invited Mahatma Gandhi (Lucknow Session 1916) against Tinkathia system (3/20 land). Companions: Rajendra Prasad, Brajkishore Prasad, J.B. Kripalani, Anugrah Narayan Sinha.
  • • Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha (Nov 1929): Founded by Swami Sahajanand Saraswati at Sonepur Fair; All India Kisan Sabha formed in 1936 (Lucknow).
  • • Bakasht Movement (1936–38): Led by Karyanand Sharma (Munger/Barahiya Tal), Rahul Sankrityayan, and Yadunandan Sharma.
Congress Sessions & 1942

INC Sessions in Bihar & Quit India

  • • 1912 Bankipore (Patna) Session: 27th INC Session; President: R.N. Mudholkar.
  • • 1922 Gaya Session: 37th INC Session; President: Chittaranjan Das (C.R. Das) — led to formation of Swaraj Party (1923).
  • • 11 August 1942 (Patna Secretariat Firing): 7 student martyrs shot while hoisting the Tricolour (ordered by District Magistrate W.G. Archer).
  • • Azad Dasta (1942): Guerrilla force formed by Jayaprakash Narayan in Nepal (Bakri Ka Tapu) after escaping Hazaribagh Jail on Diwali night.
Bihar Panchayati Raj Act 2006

Three-Tier Structure & Quotas

  • • 50% Reservation for Women: Bihar became the 1st state in India (2006 Act) to grant 50% reservation to women across all 3 tiers.
  • • Gram Sabha Quorum: Minimum 1/20th (5%) of total members; at least 4 meetings mandatory per year (max gap 3 months).
  • • Gram Katchahry: Headed by Sarpanch with Nyaya Mitras & Panchs; jurisdiction over minor civil suits up to ₹10,000.
Saat Nischay-2 (2020–2025)

7 Resolves of Good Governance

  • • 1. Yuva Shakti — Bihar Ki Pragati: Mega skill centers, ITIs, polytechnics & entrepreneurship support.
  • • 2. Sashakt Mahila, Saksham Mahila: Chief Minister Mahila Udyami Yojana (up to ₹10 lakh: 50% grant + 50% interest-free/low-interest loan).
  • • 3–7: Har Khet Tak Sinchai Ka Pani • Swachh Gaon Samriddh Gaon (Solar street lights) • Swachh Shahar • Sulabh Samparkta • Sabke Liye Atirikt Swasthya Suvidha.
Constitutional Committees

Panchayati Raj Evolution

  • • Balwant Rai Mehta (1957): 3-tier Panchayati Raj (launched 2 Oct 1959 in Nagaur, Rajasthan).
  • • Ashok Mehta (1977): Recommended 2-tier system (Mandal Panchayat & Zila Parishad).
  • • L.M. Singhvi (1986): Recommended Constitutional status for Panchayats.
  • • 73rd Amendment Act (1992): Added Part IX (Articles 243 to 243-O) & 11th Schedule (29 functional items); effective 24 April 1993.
Fundamental Rights & DPSP

Must-Know Articles (12–51A)

  • • Art 14–18 (Equality): Art 17 = Abolition of Untouchability; Art 18 = Abolition of Titles.
  • • Art 19–22 (Freedom): Art 21 = Right to Life & Personal Liberty; Art 21A = Free Education (6–14 yrs, 86th Amendment 2002).
  • • Art 32 & 226 (Writs): Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo Warranto.
  • • Key DPSPs: Art 40 (Village Panchayats), Art 44 (Uniform Civil Code), Art 50 (Separation of Judiciary from Executive).
Union & State Executive

Parliament, President & Governor

  • • President: Art 52 (Office), Art 60 (Oath), Art 61 (Impeachment), Art 72 (Pardoning Power), Art 123 (Ordinance).
  • • Governor: Art 153 (Office), Art 161 (Pardoning Power), Art 200 (Assent to Bills), Art 213 (Ordinance).
  • • Parliament & Finance: Art 108 (Joint Sitting), Art 110 (Money Bill), Art 112 (Annual Financial Statement / Budget), Art 280 (Finance Commission).
Constitutional Bodies

Key Institutions & Amendments

  • • Key Bodies: Art 76 (Attorney General), Art 148 (CAG), Art 165 (Advocate General), Art 315 (UPSC/BPSC), Art 324 (Election Commission).
  • • Landmark Amendments: 42nd (1976 — Socialist, Secular, Integrity & Fundamental Duties), 44th (1978 — Right to Property made legal right under Art 300A), 61st (1989 — Voting age 21 to 18), 101st (GST).
Physics High-Yield

Optics, Waves & SI Units

  • • Total Internal Reflection (TIR): Optical fiber, sparkling of diamond, mirage in desert, endoscope.
  • • Lenses & Defects: Myopia (short-sightedness) → Concave lens; Hypermetropia (far-sightedness) → Convex lens; Presbyopia → Bifocal lens.
  • • Sound vs Light: Sound is longitudinal mechanical wave (fastest in solids, zero in vacuum); Light is transverse electromagnetic wave (fastest in vacuum: 3×10⁸ m/s).
Chemistry High-Yield

Acids, Common Salts & Alloys

  • • Natural Acids: Vinegar (Acetic), Ant/Nettle sting (Formic/Methanoic), Tomato/Spinach (Oxalic), Tamarind/Grapes (Tartaric), Curd (Lactic).
  • • Common Compounds: Baking Soda (NaHCO₃), Washing Soda (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O), Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O), Bleaching Powder (CaOCl₂).
  • • Key Alloys: Brass (Cu + Zn), Bronze (Cu + Sn), Solder (Pb + Sn), Stainless Steel (Fe + Cr + Ni + C).
Biology High-Yield

Cell Organelles, Vitamins & Glands

  • • Cell Organelles: Mitochondria (Powerhouse, has own DNA), Ribosome (Protein factory), Lysosome (Suicidal bag), Chloroplast (Kitchen of plant cell).
  • • Solubility: Water-soluble vitamins = Vitamin B & C; Fat-soluble = Vitamin A, D, E, K.
  • • Blood & Glands: Universal Donor = O⁻; Universal Recipient = AB⁺; Largest gland = Liver; Master gland = Pituitary; Mixed gland = Pancreas (Insulin from Beta cells).
